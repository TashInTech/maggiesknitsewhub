About us

Mission

To empower individuals from all walks of life — especially the marginalised — through accessible, inclusive training in knitting and sewing.

We believe everyone deserves the chance to express their creativity and develop skills that build confidence and improve livelihoods.

Through a nurturing environment, we inspire participants to express themselves through craft while fostering belonging and community.

Vision

A world where knitting and sewing are not just hobbies but pathways to empowerment and self-sufficiency for everyone, regardless of background.

A thriving community where people develop their talents, gain economic independence, and contribute to the local economy through handmade creations.

By bridging skill development and personal growth, we cultivate a culture of creativity, resilience and support.

Objectives

Provide practical training in knitting and sewing.

Equip marginalised groups with income-generating skills.

Reduce unemployment and poverty in the target community.

Promote self-reliance and entrepreneurship among beneficiaries.

Foster social inclusion through a welcoming, shared space.

Target beneficiaries

  1. Orphans
  2. Young mothers
  3. School dropouts
  4. Youth
  5. Other marginalised and vulnerable individuals
  6. Anyone curious about knitting and sewing

What we teach

Structured, practical training — not just a one-off class.

Activities

  • Training sessions in knitting
  • Training sessions in sewing
  • Mentorship and entrepreneurship guidance
  • Practical production workshops
  • Community outreach and beneficiary recruitment

Why choose us

At Skill Bridge Hub Initiative, we believe in fostering creativity and building skills in a supportive environment. We pride ourselves on a vibrant, community-based approach that empowers individuals of all skill levels to come together, learn and share their passion for knitting and sewing. With hands-on workshops led by experienced instructors, participants gain practical skills while building lasting friendships. Whether you're training for a trade or learning for yourself, our supportive environment encourages creativity and collaboration, making each session an inspiring step into the world of textiles.
